Getting Started

What is TerraMine?
TerraMine is a GPS-based mobile game where every property you buy — a TerraAcre (TA) — is tied to a real-world location. Each one generates TerraBucks (TB) and a trickle of passive USD income over time. Walk around, claim unclaimed grid squares near you, and run their daily mini-games to level them up. See the full Player's Guide for a step-by-step walkthrough.
How do I claim a mine?
Open the map, walk to an unclaimed grid square near you, tap it, and pay 100 TB to claim it. It's randomly assigned one of four mine types — Rock, Coal, Gold, or Diamond — each with its own earning rate and mini-game.
Is TerraMine free to play?
Yes. TerraMine is free to download and play, with optional rewarded ads that boost your earnings.

Currencies & Cash Out

What's the difference between TerraBucks and my USD earnings?
TerraBucks (TB) is the in-game currency you use to claim and upgrade mines. Your USD earnings build up passively from your mines over time in real dollars, and can be traded for TB or eventually cashed out.
How do I trade my USD earnings for TB?
Tap your USD balance on the map screen and choose "Trade Earnings for TB." The standard rate is 200 TB per $1, with a bonus rate of 300 TB per $1 on the 1st and 15th of each month.
Can I cash out my USD earnings for real money?
Cash Out via PayPal is launching in August 2026. We'll announce it in-app and on Discord as soon as it's live — no action needed on your end until then.
Is there a fee to cash out, and does it depend on my country?
Yes — a withdrawal exchange fee applies and it varies by country tier, from 0% up to 38%. See Withdrawal Exchange Rates for the full country-by-country breakdown. These rates are reviewed periodically and subject to change, so check that page for the current rate before withdrawing.

Ads

An ad won't play, or gets stuck loading — what do I do?
We don't have control over the behavior of individual ads — that's determined by our ad network, and availability can vary by device, region, and time of day. If an ad gets stuck, force-close and reopen the app; that resolves it most of the time. Reliability keeps improving with every update.

Account & Verification

Phone verification says I've hit a limit — what now?
We're actively working with our verification provider to raise the daily limit. Try again in a day or two and it should go through. If it still doesn't work after that, email us at scott@terramine.app.
What happens if I stop playing for a while?
Individual mines that go untouched for an extended period get flagged and can have their passive earnings paused — you'll see a warning in-app well before that happens. Your account itself follows a similar staged warning process for long-term inactivity. Full details are in our Terms of Service. Your TerraBucks and USD balance are never deleted.

Referrals

How do referral codes work?
Share your referral code with a friend. When they enter it during signup, they get 2,000 TB and you get 1,000 TB. It has to be entered at signup — it can't be applied to an account that already exists.
I referred a friend but didn't get my bonus — what happened?
First, double check your friend entered your code at signup, not afterward. Rewards are also capped per day to prevent abuse, so a burst of referrals in one day may spill over to the next. If it's been a couple of days and you still don't see it, email scott@terramine.app with both your referral code and your friend's — that's the fastest way for us to look it up.

Check-ins & Mines

What happens when I check in to someone else's mine?
You can leave the owner a message and photo, and they'll see it in their Visitor Log. Checking into multiple mines in one day also builds a streak bonus.
Do I need to play a mine's mini-game every day?
Not required, but it keeps that mine's activity level up and unlocks higher rewards as you level it. Each of the four mine types — Rock, Coal, Gold, and Diamond — has its own mini-game. See the Player's Guide for details on each.
