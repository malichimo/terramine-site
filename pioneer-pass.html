Questions

Pioneer Pass FAQ

How much does it cost?

$9.99 a month, or $100 a year (about 17% less than paying monthly). Both are billed through PayPal.

When can I sign up?

Right now. Choose monthly or yearly, enter your referral code and subscribe with PayPal. We add the pass to your account, usually within a day. Your free vanity referral code can be claimed right away on the Account page of the web portal. On iPhone, the in-app perks (7×7 range and Pioneer Boosts) appear with the next App Store update.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime from your PayPal account. Your perks stay on until the end of the month or year you've paid for.

What happens to my custom referral code if I cancel?

You keep it. Changing it later works the same as for any player (5,000 TB, once a month).

Can I buy Pioneer Pass inside the app?

No. Pioneer Pass is only available here on terramine.app.

Do I need the latest version of the app?

Yes. Make sure TerraMine is up to date from the App Store or Google Play to see every perk.

I paid but don't see my perks.

Close and reopen the app. If they're still missing, email scott@terramine.app with your referral code and we'll sort it out.
