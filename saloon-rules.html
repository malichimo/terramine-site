Daily Spin

How it works

  • Spin the wheel for a prize. A spin never costs you TB.
  • You get 1 free spin a day. You can earn up to 2 more by watching a rewarded ad, so up to 3 spins a day.
  • A day runs on Eastern Time (America/New_York) and resets at midnight. Unused spins don't carry over.
  • The wheel has 8 segments. Prizes are TB or in-game items (such as materials or an Ad Skip). Each segment is shown on the wheel.
  • The server decides where the wheel lands. Some prizes are far more common than others, and the big prizes are rare. Prizes and odds are set by TerraMine and may change.
  • Daily Spin results are not counted as a "bet," and a spin can't lose you anything.
  • Ad spins use their own ticket. An Ad Skip can't pay for a spin, and a spin ticket can't be used for anything else. If a spin fails to go through, you keep your ticket and can try again.

Roulette

The table

  • One wheel with 37 pockets: 0 and 1 to 36 (a single zero). Eighteen numbers are red, eighteen are black, and 0 is green.
  • You place chips on one or more bets, then spin. The server picks the winning pocket.
  • Your whole stake is taken when you spin. Winning bets pay back your stake plus the winnings. Losing bets are lost.
  • The bet limit applies to the total of all your bets in one spin. The minimum and maximum are shown at the table and may change.
  • There is a short wait (about 5 seconds) between spins. Fractions of a TB are rounded down.
  • Your bets stay on the table for the next spin unless you can no longer afford them. You can clear or change them at any time.
  • You may see a full-screen ad every few spins.

Blackjack

Goal

Beat the dealer's hand without going over 21. You are playing only against the dealer.

Rules at this table

  • Six decks, shuffled fresh before every hand. Counting cards doesn't help.
  • Number cards count at face value, J, Q and K count 10, and an Ace counts 11 or 1, whichever helps the hand.
  • Blackjack (an Ace and a 10-value card as your first two cards) pays 3 to 2. If you both have one, it's a push.
  • The dealer stands on every 17 (including soft 17) and must hit 16 or less.
  • The dealer checks for blackjack when showing an Ace or a 10-value card. If the dealer has it, the hand ends right away.
  • Hit to take a card, Stand to keep your total.
  • Double: on your first two cards (and after a split) you may double your bet, take exactly one more card, and stand. You need enough TB to cover it.
  • Split: when your first two cards are a pair, you may split into two hands with an equal extra bet. You can split once (into 2 hands). Split Aces get one card each. 21 after a split is not a blackjack.
  • No insurance and no surrender.
  • A winning hand pays 1 to 1. A tie (push) returns your bet. A bust loses.
  • The bet limit applies to your starting bet. Doubles and splits may take you above it.
  • You have 30 seconds to act. If time runs out, your hand stands automatically. If you leave mid-hand, the hand is stood and settled when you next open Blackjack.
  • After every 10 hands the dealer shuffles. You can leave the table or play on, and a full-screen ad plays when you continue.

With perfect basic strategy, the house edge in this game is roughly 0.5% to 0.6%. Real play is usually worse for the player than perfect strategy.
